Smart telescopes have made photographing objects in deep space much easier. But one of the most important things to do is pick targets that work well with your telescope and the sky where you are.

Some objects respond effectively to shorter exposure times. Here is a practical list of deep-sky targets particularly well-suited to a smart telescope.

|-|-|-|-|-|-|

|Andromeda Galaxy|M31|Spiral Galaxy|Andromeda|1-3 hours|6.3 * 2.1 Moons|
|Hercules Cluster|M13|Globular Cluster|Hercules|15-20 minutes|0.7 Moons wide|

#### Checklist
When selecting a target, consider the following checklist:
- [x] Object above 40<sup>o</sup> altitude ( at least for 70% of the shooting time)
- [x] No bright Moon nearby
- [x] Clear forecast and stable atmosphere
- [x] Target fits the telescope field of view
- [x] At least **60 minutes total exposure**
